Hemant Nagrale — Co-Founder & Strategic Advisor

Hemant Nagrale is a distinguished Indian Police Service (IPS) officer of the 1987 batch, Maharashtra cadre, with a highly respected career spanning several decades in law enforcement and administration. He has served in senior leadership roles across Maharashtra Police and central investigative agencies, bringing deep experience in governance, strategic decision-making, and institutional leadership.

His strong academic foundation has underpinned a remarkable career in policing and public administration.
